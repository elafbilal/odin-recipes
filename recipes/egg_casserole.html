Egg Casserole


Home

Description


This egg casserole is a hearty and delicious breakfast dish that combines eggs, cheese, vegetables, and sometimes meat. It's perfect for feeding a crowd or preparing ahead of time for busy mornings.


Ingredients


  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup shredded cheese (cheddar, mozzarella, or your choice)
  • 1 cup diced vegetables (bell peppers, onions, spinach, etc.)
  • 1/2 cup cooked meat (optional, such as bacon or sausage)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions


  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs and milk until well combined.
  3. Stir in the shredded cheese, diced vegetables, and cooked meat (if using). Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Pour the mixture into a greased baking dish.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 30-35 minutes, or until the eggs are set and the top is golden brown.
  6. Remove from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving.
